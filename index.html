Problem statement

Why we need AskFSKTM

No central Q&A platform

Past answers get lost in chats

Repeated questions go undetected

Too many apps are used

Spectrum can't answer questions

It shares materials, not answers

Lecturers reply one by one

Same answer, typed again and again

Shy students don't ask

Asking in public feels scary

No easy meeting booking

Endless emails to find a slot

Proposed system

How AskFSKTM works

1

Ask

Pick a course or General, ask the chatbot

2

AI Search

Answers from course & faculty docs first

3

Escalate

Post anonymously & pick a lecturer

4

Smart AI

Detects duplicates, filters spam, summarises

5

Reply

Lecturer replies once, with AI-drafted help

Schedule

Book slots or lecturer proposals, with reminders

Dashboards

For students, lecturers & admins (incl. support tickets)

Self-Learning

Project objectives

Project scope

What we cover

The right answers, from the right people, in the right place. A web app for FCSIT students, lecturers & admins.

In scope

  • Q&A board: course + General areas
  • Anonymous, AI-moderated posting
  • Choose a lecturer to reply
  • AI chatbot (course & faculty docs)
  • Duplicate merge & AI summaries
  • Reply once + AI draft replies
  • Pinned answers teach the chatbot
  • Meeting booking & proposals
  • Admin: users, tickets, moderation
  • Dashboards for all three roles

Out of scope

  • Mobile apps
  • Grades and marks
  • Live MAYA/Spectrum integration (mocked)
  • Announcements & timetables (Spectrum)
  • Use outside FCSIT
